## Scope and visitor mode

Operate. The shared web shell lets students, runners, and administrators enter their current task from one continuous campus-service workspace.

## Audience, job, and task

Students publish and track services, runners handle available work and earnings, and administrators govern operations. The active route, current page, and primary action must be obvious without crossing visual seams.

## Content and constraints

Preserve all Vue routes, permissions, data, terminology, and functional controls. Keep the Campus Express name. The user supplied a visual reference that pins the layout: left navigation is a full-height pale-blue field, the right side is a continuous daylight canvas, and the previous detached three-column/cards treatment is out of scope.

## Chosen direction and memorable moment

Campus Operations Canvas: opening the application feels like arriving at one complete desktop workspace, rather than a page assembled from floating dashboard panels. A pale-blue work index occupies the full left edge. A white command header names the active page and keeps the role-specific action at the top-right. The working content begins directly below on a cool white canvas with thin blue-gray rules, compact white task panels, and cobalt as the sole action signal.

## Unresolved decisions

No new brand asset is needed. Mobile keeps the same index as an off-canvas pale-blue drawer rather than converting it into unrelated chips.
